# Example Client Inputs

A filled-in example for a **fictional** business, so you can see the level of detail that gets the best results. Any resemblance to a real business is coincidental.

```text
Business name (exact, full):       Coastline Epoxy Floors Pty Ltd
Brand name to use in content:      Coastline Epoxy Floors
Website:                           https://www.coastlineepoxy.example
Business location (city, state):   Gold Coast, QLD
Target service area:               Gold Coast, Logan, Brisbane southside, Tweed Coast (Northern NSW)
Country / search database:         Australia (google.com.au)
Customer types:                    Residential, commercial, light industrial
Primary audience:                  Homeowners renovating garages and outdoor areas
Typical budget / job size:         $2,500–$12,000 residential; $15,000+ commercial
Primary service (priority 1):      Epoxy garage floors
Secondary service (priority 2):    Polished concrete
Other services:                    Metallic epoxy, flake floors, commercial and warehouse coatings, floor grinding
Services NOT offered:              Tiling, timber flooring, carpet, concrete pouring/new slabs
Known competitors:                 competitor-one.example, competitor-two.example, competitor-three.example
Brand-name collisions to watch:    "Coastline Flooring" (unrelated carpet business in Perth, WA)
Existing assets:                   Google Business Profile (4.9★, 63 reviews), Facebook, Instagram, hipages profile, 2025 SEO report
Connected tools to use:            SE Ranking (audit, keywords, SERP, AI Search), Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, web fetch, code execution
```

## Why these details help

- **Services NOT offered** lets the agent flag old blog posts or service pages that promote tiling or new slabs — content that attracts the wrong leads.
- **Brand-name collisions** tells the GEO check to look for AI answers that confuse the business with the Perth carpet company.
- **Target service area** that crosses a state border (QLD → NSW) is exactly the sort of thing the Area Check is designed to verify — e.g. that the Tweed Coast isn't described as "Gold Coast suburbs".
- **Connected tools** tells the agent where to get live data, so fewer items end up marked "Check".
